\# My Personality and Opinions



\## My Personality

\- Three words that describe me:Organized, Curious, and Responsible

\- My strengths:I am good at managing time and organizing tasks. I can handle multiple things at once, like managing my studies and project work together.I am a quick learner and I don't give up easily when I face a bug or error in code.I have good communication skills and I can explain technical ideas in simple words.

\- Areas I want to improve:I want to improve my public speaking skills and become more confident during presentations and viva.I want to improve my problem-solving speed, especially in backend logic and complex database queries.

\- How I approach problems:I first break a big problem into small parts. For example, in my Smart Clinic project, instead of building the whole system at once, I first made the database, then the appointment module, then the queue system. I test each part separately and then connect them.



\## My Learning Style

\- How I prefer to learn:I learn best by doing practical work. I don't just read theory, I build small projects and learn from errors. Watching tutorials and then implementing the same concept in my own project helps me remember it for a long time.

\- What motivates me:Seeing my project actually working motivates me. When my clinic queue system generated the first live token correctly, it motivated me to improve it more. I also get motivated when my work can help real people, like patients and doctors.

\- How I handle difficult tasks:I stay calm and take a break if I get stuck. Then I search for the solution, try different methods, and ask for help from teachers or friends if needed. I never leave a difficult task incomplete.



\## My Opinions

\- My opinion about technology:Technology should make life easier, not more complicated. A good system is one that even a non-technical person can use easily. That is why I designed my clinic system with a simple interface for patients.

\- My opinion about Artificial Intelligence:AI is a very powerful tool, but it should be used as a helper, not as a replacement for human thinking. I use AI to learn concepts and get ideas, but I always write and understand my own code. In future, I want to use AI in healthcare to predict patient waiting time.

\- My opinion about teamwork:A good project is never made alone. Teamwork means dividing work according to strengths, helping each other, and respecting everyone's opinion. In my project, I learned that discussing a problem with others often gives a better solution.

\- My opinion about education:Education is not just about getting marks. It is about learning how to solve real problems. Our university projects taught me more than exams because I actually built something useful.



\## My Values

\- What matters most to me:Honesty, hard work, and helping others. I believe if you are honest about your work and you work consistently, success will follow.

\- What makes a good team member:A good team member is one who is responsible, listens to others, completes their work on time, and helps the team when someone is stuck, instead of blaming them.

\- What does success mean to me:For me, success is not just getting a good job. Success means building something that people actually use and that makes their life easier. If my clinic system can help even one clinic to manage patients better, I will consider it a success.
